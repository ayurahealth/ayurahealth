## 2026-06-03 - Accessible Icon Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like play/pause, voice inputs) lack context for screen readers and can be confusing for sighted users. Adding dynamic 'aria-label' and 'title' attributes provides semantic meaning and tooltips.
**Action:** Always include both 'aria-label' and 'title' attributes on all icon-only buttons, ensuring they dynamically reflect the button's current action or state.
