# Ayura Intelligence — Growth & Business Strategy

## Market Position

Ayura Intelligence occupies the intersection of:
- **Traditional medicine** (underserved by Western health tech)
- **AI health assistants** (rapidly growing but mostly Western-medicine focused)
- **Multilingual health access** (50+ languages, emerging markets)

## Target Segments

### Primary (B2C)
- Health-conscious urban millennials in India, Japan, SE Asia
- Users seeking alternatives to symptom-Googling
- Ayurveda practitioners needing a digital assistant
- People with chronic conditions seeking holistic support

### Secondary (B2B)
- Ayurvedic clinics (500+ target partners)
- Wellness centers and retreat operators
- Healthcare providers offering integrative medicine

### Tertiary (Enterprise)
- Insurance companies for preventive care
- Hospital networks in India and Japan
- Corporate wellness programs

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Key Features |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Free | Rs. 0 | 10 consultations, basic dosha quiz |
| Pro | Rs. 499/mo | Unlimited chats, clinical memory, deep think, attachments, PDF analysis |
| Clinic | Rs. 4,999/mo | Multi-user, API access, white-label, priority support, analytics |

## Growth Levers

1. **SEO** — Ayurveda + AI search intent (high volume, low competition)
2. **Viral Chat Sharing** — Shareable health insight cards with VAIDYA citation
3. **Clinic Partnerships** — B2B sales with revenue share model
4. **Content Marketing** — Traditional medicine knowledge base and blog
5. **Community** — Reddit r/AyuraIntelligence + Discord
6. **Multilingual** — Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic content for each market

## Go-To-Market Phases

### Phase 1: Validation (Current — Q2 2026)
- Free tier live at ayurahealth.com
- Target: 10,000 MAU, 100 Pro subscribers
- KPI: Chat session completion rate > 70%
- Focus: Product-market fit, NPS > 40

### Phase 2: Monetization (Q3 2026)
- Launch Pro tier marketing aggressively
- Clinic partnership beta program (50 clinics)
- iOS App Store launch
- Target: Rs. 5L MRR (~$6K)

### Phase 3: Scale (Q4 2026 – Q2 2027)
- Enterprise API product launch
- Japan market localization + marketing
- Referral and affiliate program
- Target: $1M ARR

## Competitive Advantages

1. **8-tradition synthesis** — No competitor offers cross-system medical depth
2. **Classical citations** — Trust and authority from 5,000-year-old texts
3. **Privacy-first** — No health data sold, HIPAA-compatible trajectory
4. **Multilingual** — 50+ languages natively supported
5. **Speed** — Groq-powered < 1.5s first token (fastest in class)
6. **Free tier generosity** — 10 full consultations before paywall

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| AI hallucination in medical context | Citation-gated responses, quality scoring, disclaimers |
| Regulatory (medical advice) | "Educational purposes only" framing, safety pages |
| Groq API downtime | OpenRouter fallback chain |
| DB outage | Graceful degradation (no crash), connection pooling |
| Key misuse | Rate limiting, auth guards, webhook verification |
