# Ayura Intelligence — Product Goals

## Mission
To build the world's most authoritative clinical AI for traditional medicine — making 5,000 years of healing wisdom accessible, personalized, and scientifically grounded for every human on Earth.

## Vision
> "Ancient Wisdom. Neural Synthesis. Modern Healing."

Ayura Intelligence bridges classical medical texts (Charaka Samhita, Huangdi Neijing, Sowa Rigpa, etc.) with modern AI reasoning to provide institutional-grade health guidance.

## North Star Metrics

| Metric | Target (12 months) |
|--------|--------------------|
| Monthly Active Users | 100,000 |
| Chat Consultations / Day | 50,000 |
| Clinic Partners | 500 |
| Response Quality Score | > 0.85 / 1.0 |
| Uptime SLA | 99.9% |

## Strategic Goals

### G1 — Clinical Accuracy
- All AI responses grounded in classical citations
- Dosha-based personalization for every user
- Response quality auto-scoring with feedback loops

### G2 — Accessibility
- 50+ languages via multilingual orchestration
- Free tier with generous limits
- Mobile-first (iOS via Capacitor + PWA)

### G3 — Trust & Safety
- Medical disclaimer on every response
- No hallucination tolerance — citation-gated reasoning
- HIPAA-compatible data handling

### G4 — Sustainable Revenue
- Free → Pro → Clinic tier model
- Razorpay-powered billing (INR-native)
- API access for clinic integrations

### G5 — Developer Velocity
- Zero-downtime deployments via Vercel
- AI-assisted code review in CI pipeline
- Full TypeScript type safety throughout

## Non-Goals (v1)
- Replacing licensed medical practitioners
- Storing identifiable health records (HIPAA phase 2)
- Real-time video consultations
- Wearable device integrations
